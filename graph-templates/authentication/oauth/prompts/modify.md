To add or remove a provider or an allowed post-login path, re-render with new inputs and review the proposal rather than editing `src/config/oauthProviders.ts` by hand. Keep the account directory adapter's contract: never report `emailVerified: true` for an address the application has not verified, and never create an account without the provider-verified email passed in. Do not add request-supplied identity fields, extra redirect targets or logging of provider responses to the service, and do not weaken ID token signature verification: keys come only from the configured `jwksUri`, and only RS256 and ES256 are accepted.
